Website redesign and custom interface

Porch Stomp

A clearer digital home for a growing New York City folk festival.

Visit live workTalk about similar work

The situation

A growing festival had outgrown a collection of pages.

Porch Stomp needed visitors to understand the event, find artists and stages, participate, support the festival, and explore past years without learning the site’s internal history first.

The challenge was not simply visual. The information needed a clearer hierarchy and a structure that could keep growing.

What changed

Navigation

Grouped the site around the questions a visitor actually arrives with.

Mobile readability

Reduced dense blocks, strengthened hierarchy, and designed controls for smaller screens.

Lineup discovery

Created a lineup interface that supports browsing now and a searchable archive later.

Participation

Made artist, volunteer, support, and mailing-list paths easier to find.

Result

A clearer festival experience that supports discovery now and a growing archive later.
