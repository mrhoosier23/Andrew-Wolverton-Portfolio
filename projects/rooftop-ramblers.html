Live music booking and human-reviewed workflow

Rooftop Ramblers

I built a booking system that gathers scattered venue facts while keeping fit and outreach decisions human.

AI & workflow services & pricing

The situation

Booking a band involves repeated research that still needs human judgment.

Venue websites, Instagram profiles, booking emails, location details, genres, capacity, and past outreach often live in different places. I built the system to gather the facts, then I decide whether the venue is a real fit and whether outreach should be sent.

What comes inScattered venue information

I gather websites, social profiles, booking contacts, location, genre, and prior outreach.

What happensResearch and qualification

I check likely venues, flag missing details, and put the useful facts together.

Human decisionAndrew approves the fit

I do not send anything automatically. I make the final outreach decision.

What comes outA booking board

I keep ready-to-pitch venues, replies, follow-up dates, and next actions visible.

Result

The result is less repeated searching and a clearer booking pipeline, while I keep the final call human.
