B2B website strategy and buyer journey

Yolélé Ingredients

I reorganized Yolélé Ingredients around what commercial buyers need to evaluate products, sourcing, applications, and scale.

Visit live workWebsite services & pricing

The situation

A commercial buyer needed to understand the opportunity quickly.

Yolélé Ingredients serves foodservice and processor audiences evaluating fonio and other West African ingredients. The site needed to explain products, formats, applications, sourcing, traceability, and scale without relying on broad brand language.

How I reorganized the buyer journey

What is the product?

I led with a clear ingredient overview and practical specifications.

How can it be used?

I connected ingredients to real food and beverage applications.

Can the supply support us?

I explained sourcing, processing, traceability, and commercial scale.

What happens next?

I built the inquiry around the information needed for a useful, qualified lead.

Result

The result is a clearer route from trade-show interest and networking conversations to product evaluation and a useful sales inquiry.
