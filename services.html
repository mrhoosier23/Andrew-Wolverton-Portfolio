What working together looks like

Clear enough to keep moving, flexible enough to respond.

  1. 01Listen

    Understand the audience, the real problem, what already exists, and the outcome that matters.

  2. 02Map

    Choose priorities, content, pathways, dependencies, and the smartest version to build first.

  3. 03Make

    Build the website, campaign, edit, performance plan, or working system.

  4. 04Test

    Check clarity, accessibility, playback, responsive behavior, and technical reliability.

  5. 05Hand off

    Deliver usable work with documentation, context, decisions, and a clear next step.

Service questions

Useful answers before the first call.

Do I need a complete brief?

No. Bring the problem, the audience, what already exists, and any deadline or budget limits you know.

Can we start smaller?

Yes. An audit, sprint, prototype, or first-phase build can create a useful stopping point before a larger engagement.

Do you work inside existing platforms?

Yes. I work in Wix, WordPress, Google Workspace, common marketing platforms, audio and video tools, and custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript when appropriate.

Can services be combined?

Yes. A website may need campaign copy, video, forms, or workflow planning. The scope should follow the real project rather than force the work into separate silos.
