## Reply when a leader asks what teachers need beforehand

Teachers need a laptop, access to the school-approved AI environment if one exists, and one recurring task they personally perform. They do not need AI experience, coding, a prepared prompt, or student records. I handle the build structure and fictional practice pack. Before the session, I spend 45 minutes with leadership confirming the approved tool, local rules, and escalation contact.

## Reply when a leader asks about safety

The workshop is designed to avoid student records entirely. Teachers practice recognizing direct and indirect identifiers, use blank, public, fictional, or student-neutral sources, and build explicit refusal behavior into each assistant. The school retains responsibility for tool approval and local policy; the teacher retains every instructional and final-use decision. If the approved environment is unclear, we run the lab in paper/demo mode rather than creating a workaround account.

## Reply when a leader asks about outcomes

Each teacher leaves with one assistant brief, approved and prohibited input lists, copy-ready instructions, a refusal rule, one fictional test, a four-point human-review card, and a two-week measurement log. At follow-up, each participant chooses keep, revise, or stop based on actual time, correction work, usefulness, and safety slips. I do not promise a fixed number of hours saved.
