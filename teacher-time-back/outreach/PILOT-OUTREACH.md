I am reaching out with a deliberately narrow professional-learning pilot: help teachers stop rebuilding one recurring piece of work without turning the session into a generic AI overview.

The Safe AI Assistant Lab begins with the school's approved environment and local rules. Teachers choose one lower-risk task, map safe inputs, build one assistant blueprint, test it with fictional material, and practice a four-point human review. The $1,500 founding pilot supports 8-20 educators and includes leadership setup, a 90-minute lab, all materials, and a two-week follow-up focused on measured usefulness, correction time, and safety questions.
