## Professional-learning or instructional-technology lead

**Subject:** A teacher-facing AI pilot built around one repeated workload

Hi [Name],

Teacher Time Back is not an AI overview or prompt library. Teachers build one reusable workflow around work they already do.

The flagship example is a Weekly Lesson Setup Assistant. Teachers can bring an objective, notes, old materials, screenshots, links, rough ideas, or an unfinished deck. They teach the assistant the process, test it, correct it, and reuse it. UDL/accessibility and teaching-material options are added inside the same workflow rather than treated as separate modules.

A short family-communication example then shows how the method transfers: clarify the source message, protect exact details, draft, verify, review, and follow the school's approved language-access process.
