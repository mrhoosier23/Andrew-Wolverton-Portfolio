# Teacher Time Back Lab offer contract

## Public promise

**Give teachers back time.**

Turn one repetitive part of a teacher's weekly workload into a reusable AI-assisted workflow in 90 minutes.

Teacher Time Back is a teacher-workflow improvement program that uses AI. The AI is the mechanism. Recovered teacher time is the product.

The assistant prepares. The teacher decides.

## Flagship build

The first lab stays deliberately narrow. Teachers build one **Weekly Lesson Setup Assistant** rather than moving through a catalog of unrelated AI tools.

Teachers may bring an objective, curriculum or context, grade, constraints, notes, old materials, screenshots, links, rough ideas, or an unfinished deck. A polished lesson is not required.

The saved workflow can help organize:

- lesson structure and sequence
- UDL and accessibility options
- differentiation without lowering the instructional goal
- teaching-deck outline
- checks for understanding
- exit ticket or other supporting materials
- decisions that still require teacher input

The educator remains responsible for accuracy, rigor, appropriateness, accessibility, student fit, and final instructional decisions.

## Transfer example

Family communication is a short transfer example, not a second workshop.

The same workflow-building method can be demonstrated as:

1. Clarify the source message into plain language.
2. Identify protected names, dates, deadlines, links, locations, and other details that must remain exact.
3. Prepare a translation-ready or multilingual draft.
4. Verify protected details against the original.
5. Teacher reviews accuracy, tone, and appropriateness.
6. Use the school's approved language-access process.

The lab does not replace required translation, interpretation, language-access, privacy, or human-review processes.

## Readiness gate

The school names the exact AI product, staff account type, local rules, and escalation contact before the lab is booked. A school without a selected tool receives a separate readiness conversation. Participants never create workaround accounts.

## Introductory boundary

The lab uses no real student information, student work, family records, personnel records, or confidential school files. Replacing names with initials, Student A, group labels, or key IDs does not automatically make information safe.

Grading, discipline, placement, intervention, accommodation, eligibility, counseling, employee evaluation, and other consequential decisions remain outside the introductory assistant.

## Participant outcome

Each teacher leaves with:

- one saved five-part assistant setup
- one tested lesson-planning workflow
- one teacher correction
- one review routine
- a clear reuse plan
- a baseline for how long the recurring task normally takes
- a 2–4 week keep, revise, or stop test

No fixed time saving, compliance certification, legal determination, or student-level outcome is promised.
