# Teacher Time Back Lab offer contract

## Promise

Bring one task you keep doing. Leave with one assistant you can use again.

The assistant prepares. The teacher decides.

## Founding-school pilot

- $1,500
- 8–20 educators
- 45-minute leadership setup
- 90-minute teacher lab
- digital participant materials
- 30-minute follow-up two weeks later
- NYC in person or live virtual
- second same-day cohort: $750

## Readiness gate

The school names the exact AI product, staff account type, local rules, and escalation contact before the lab is booked. A school without a selected tool receives a separate readiness conversation. Participants never create workaround accounts.

## Introductory boundary

The lab uses no real student information, student work, family records, personnel records, or confidential school files. Replacing names with initials, Student A, group labels, or key IDs does not automatically make information safe.

## Participant outcome

Each teacher leaves with one saved five-part assistant setup, one practice result, one teacher correction, a five-question review card, and a two-week keep, revise, or stop test.

No fixed time saving, compliance certification, legal determination, or student-level outcome is promised.
