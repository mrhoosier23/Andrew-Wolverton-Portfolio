# Teacher Time-Back Lab: Launch Checklist

Use this checklist after the branch is merged or deployed. Andrew keeps control of every contact and sends every message himself.

## 1. Five-minute public-page check

- Open `https://www.awolverton.com/ai-schools.html` in a private browser window.
- Confirm the hero says “Give teachers time back. One task at a time.”
- Open the product explorer and all five workshop steps.
- Complete all four safety-rehearsal questions.
- Open the pilot details and verify the founding rate is $1,500.
- Open the resource hub and Time-Back Finder.
- Download the pilot overview and one participant PDF.
- Test the contact link without sending a message.

## 2. Choose the first outreach group

Start with 8-12 people who can make or influence a professional-learning decision:

- A principal, assistant principal, or instructional leader Andrew already knows.
- A district or charter-network professional-learning lead.
- A technology or innovation leader who can name the approved AI environment.
- A school-based teacher leader who can make a warm introduction.

Do not start with a mass list. The founding pilot is a learning offer, so early conversations should be specific enough to reveal objections, language, and purchasing constraints.

## 3. Prepare each message

- Choose the matching draft in `outreach/PILOT-OUTREACH.md`.
- Replace every bracketed field.
- Add one true sentence explaining why this person or school is relevant.
- Link to `https://www.awolverton.com/ai-schools.html`.
- Attach `output/pdf/Teacher-Time-Back-Lab-Pilot-Overview.pdf` only when an attachment is useful.
- Record the message in `outreach/PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv` before sending.

## 4. When someone replies

- Use `outreach/FOLLOW-UP.md` for the appropriate response.
- Ask who owns the approved-AI-environment decision.
- Confirm cohort size, format, date range, and purchasing process.
- Do not promise a fixed number of saved hours.
- Send `output/pdf/Leadership-Setup-Guide.pdf` before the setup conversation.
- Record objections and exact buyer language in the tracker.

## 5. Before accepting a pilot date

- Confirm one cohort of 8-20 educators.
- Confirm NYC in-person or live virtual delivery.
- Confirm the $1,500 founding-pilot rate and any second-cohort or travel scope.
- Name the school-approved AI environment and account, or agree to paper/demo mode.
- Name the local privacy, technology, or instructional contact for regulated questions.
- Confirm that no student records or identifiers will be used.
- Confirm the 45-minute leadership setup, 90-minute lab, and 30-minute follow-up.

## 6. After each pilot

- Collect the two-week keep/revise/stop results.
- Record the task type, frequency, minutes before and after, corrections, and unsafe-input attempts.
- Ask permission before quoting or identifying a participant or school.
- Revise the offer only from documented patterns, not one enthusiastic or frustrated reaction.
- After three pilots, review the rate, cohort size, strongest task lanes, and most common objections.
