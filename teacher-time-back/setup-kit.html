School setup kit

One setup. One lab. One useful assistant.

Each teacher leaves with one assistant blueprint for one recurring task, a safe-input routine, a fictional test, and a human-review checklist. The pilot does not require student records or automate student-level decisions.

Leadership setup, 45 minutes

Name the environment

Identify the approved AI tool and account. If none is approved, run the workshop as a paper or fictional-material design lab.

Draw the boundary

Define allowed, uncertain, and prohibited information, plus the people who answer regulated questions.

Plan the response

Decide how teachers report concerning output or accidental disclosure and where approved blueprints live.

Task traffic light

Green

  • Reuse approved lesson structures
  • Draft neutral directions
  • Prepare agendas and action lists
  • Organize teacher-owned resources

Pause and check

  • Translation and accessibility
  • Differentiation for actual groups
  • Feedback frameworks
  • Licensed curriculum

Not in this pilot

  • Identifiable student work or records
  • Automated grading
  • IEP, 504, behavior, discipline, eligibility, or placement decisions
  • Private health, family, or personnel records

Teacher lab, 90 minutes

  1. Recognize, 10 minutes: identify one recurring task and how often it happens.
  2. Choose, 10 minutes: screen for frequency, ownership, permitted inputs, and a visible output.
  3. Protect, 15 minutes: confirm the environment and replace identifiers with fictional or neutral material.
  4. Build, 25 minutes: define role, sources, output, refusal rules, and human decisions.
  5. Practice, 20 minutes: run a fictional test, inspect the result, and revise.
  6. Review, 10 minutes: check accuracy, bias, appropriateness, and alignment.

Measure learning, not output volume

Two-week pilot log

  • Minutes before and after
  • Number of uses
  • Useful, partly useful, or discarded
  • Corrections required
  • Unsafe-input attempts
  • Keep, revise, or stop

Do not promise a fixed time saving. Use the pilot to learn which task, setup, and review routine actually help in this school.
