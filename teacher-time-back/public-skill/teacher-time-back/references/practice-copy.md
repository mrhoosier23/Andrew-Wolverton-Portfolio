# Practice copy

Use the Weekly Lesson Setup example when the educator does not have a safe practice case.

- Topic: introducing renewable and nonrenewable resources
- Time: 45 minutes
- Materials: chart paper, markers, six picture cards
- Sequence idea: quick sort, brief explanation, partner re-sort, exit note
- Missing information: the teacher has not supplied the exact objective wording

The result must mark the missing objective as NEEDS TEACHER INPUT. No real student, staff, family, or school record appears in the example.
