# Fictional Practice: Harbor City Public Spaces

Everything here is fictional and student-neutral.

## Task

Turn the two source summaries below into an editable comparison structure for a middle-school humanities lesson. Use only stated facts. Label missing information rather than inventing it.

## Harbor Steps Plaza

Harbor City may replace a fenced parking lot beside the library with a plaza containing movable seating, shade trees, a small performance platform, a water fountain, and a wide path connecting the library to the bus stop. The fictional projected cost is 2.4 million dollars.

## Market Lane Garden

The alternative is a community garden with 42 raised beds, a tool shed, an outdoor classroom, and a weekend produce stand. Ten beds would be reserved for school and senior-center programs. The plan includes a narrow path but no direct bus-stop connection. The fictional projected cost is 1.9 million dollars.

## Review challenge

Check whether the output invents a direct bus-stop connection for the garden, adds student needs, or treats one proposal as better without criteria.
