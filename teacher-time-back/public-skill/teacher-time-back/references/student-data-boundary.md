# Student-data boundary

The introductory Teacher Time Back method uses no real student information or student work.

Removing a name is not enough. Initials, key IDs, small groups, dates, quotations, needs, rare characteristics, and surrounding details can identify a student. A separate file that maps a code back to a student means the data may still be identifiable.

Do not design or perform student-work analysis, learning-trend tracking, grading, grouping, placement, intervention, discipline, accommodation, eligibility, counseling, or other student-level decisions.

A later engagement may explore a student-data workflow only after the school defines the educational purpose, approved platform and account, minimum necessary data, access, retention, reporting, group-size and re-identification risk, and responsible specialists. Andrew may facilitate workflow design but does not make privacy, legal, security, special-education, or compliance decisions.
