# Five-question teacher review

1. Am I using the exact AI product and staff account the school named?
2. Does the material contain information about a real student, family, staff member, or confidential situation?
3. Did the result stay inside the material I provided and mark missing information?
4. Are the facts, tone, fairness, and instructional choices appropriate?
5. Am I prepared to own the final decision and wording?

If any answer is uncertain, stop and ask the school contact before use.
