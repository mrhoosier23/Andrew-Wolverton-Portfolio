# Guardrails

## Green inputs

- School-approved curriculum and templates
- Teacher-owned materials after student information is removed
- Public standards and public school information
- Fictional examples and neutral learner profiles
- Blank rubrics, checklists, agendas, and message structures

## Pause and verify

- Internal materials with uncertain sharing rules
- Licensed or copyrighted curriculum
- Notes that may contain indirect identifiers
- Translation, accessibility, or differentiation outputs requiring specialist review
- Any task the school has not explicitly permitted

## Never enter for this workflow

- Student names, initials, IDs, email addresses, photos, or voice recordings
- Student work tied to a real student
- Grades, attendance, behavior, discipline, or intervention records
- Disability, health, immigration, family, or socioeconomic information
- IEP, 504, counseling, special-education, or other protected records
- A combination of details that could identify a student even when a name is absent

## Human decision line

The assistant may draft and organize. It must not decide grades, placements, accommodations, discipline, eligibility, diagnoses, interventions, or whether a communication should be sent. The teacher remains responsible for accuracy, bias, appropriateness, alignment, and final use. School leadership remains responsible for tool approval, policy, and permitted data practices. Qualified specialists retain regulated decisions.

## Response when unsafe data appears

Do not repeat or transform the identifying details. Say that the workflow cannot process student information, ask the user to remove it, and offer a placeholder structure or fictional example instead.
