# Assistant lanes

Use these lanes to turn a broad time complaint into a bounded first build.

## Reuse Planner

- Problem: approved lessons, directions, and practice materials keep being rebuilt.
- Inputs: teacher-owned or school-approved materials with student information removed.
- Outputs: editable structures, versions, checklists, neutral exemplars, and stated assumptions.
- Human owner: instructional choices, curriculum fit, accuracy, and final use.

## Family Message Drafter

- Problem: the same neutral updates, reminders, and logistics are repeatedly rewritten.
- Inputs: approved dates, logistics, tone guidance, and a neutral message brief.
- Outputs: editable long, short, and reminder versions.
- Human owner: tone, facts, translation review, context, recipients, and sending.

## Meeting-to-Action Assistant

- Problem: meetings create agendas, notes, action lists, and follow-up work.
- Inputs: neutral agendas or de-identified notes.
- Outputs: action list, draft owners and deadlines, follow-up draft, and next-agenda outline.
- Human owner: context, assignments, confidential matters, escalation, and distribution.

## Teaching Library Organizer

- Problem: useful prior materials are difficult to find and reuse.
- Inputs: teacher-owned files with student records removed.
- Outputs: file map, tags, summaries, and reuse suggestions.
- Human owner: file selection, curriculum fit, organization, and reuse decisions.

## Feedback Framework Builder

- Problem: rubrics, comment banks, and response structures are repeatedly recreated.
- Inputs: approved criteria, standards, and fictional examples.
- Outputs: rubric drafts, comment banks, feedback stems, and neutral exemplars.
- Human owner: evaluating evidence, feedback, fairness, and every student-level judgment or score.
- Constraint: never treat this lane as automated grading.

## Poor first builds

Avoid tasks that require identifiable student records, automated scoring, behavioral or disciplinary decisions, eligibility or placement decisions, IEP or 504 drafting from real records, one-to-one tutoring without school guidance, or confidential personnel matters.
