# PDF distribution guide

## Before or immediately after a fit conversation

### Teacher Time Back Lab Pilot Overview

- Recipient: principal, assistant principal, professional-learning lead, instructional technology lead, or responsible-AI lead
- Purpose: understand the outcome, prerequisites, schedule, cohort, price, and next step
- Use: attach to a concise email and link to the sales page

### Leadership Readiness Guide

- Recipient: school decision-maker and designated platform or policy contact
- Purpose: identify the exact tool/account, local rules, responsibilities, logistics, and boundary
- Use: during the 45-minute setup conversation; do not send as a generic public download

## After booking

### Teacher Preparation Sheet

- Recipient: every participant, copied to the school host
- Purpose: explain what to bring, what not to bring, and what participants leave with
- Use: send 5–7 days before the lab and again with the calendar reminder

### Teacher Workbook

- Recipient: every participant
- Purpose: guide the six steps and hold the two-week use log
- Use: distribute at the start of the lab in print or Drive

### Weekly Lesson Setup Practice Pack

- Recipient: every participant
- Purpose: show rough notes, saved instructions, first result, and teacher correction using no real people or records
- Use: Andrew demonstrates it first; participants use it for the first test

### Safety and Review Card

- Recipient: every participant and the school host
- Purpose: distinguish ready, ask, and stop situations and keep the five-question teacher review visible
- Use: print as a desk reference and keep beside the AI tool during practice

### Platform Setup Record

- Recipient: Andrew, the school host, and the platform/policy contact
- Purpose: record the exact product, account, features, restrictions, and pre-session test
- Use: complete before delivery; create a new copy for every school

## Andrew and approved co-facilitators only

### Facilitator Playbook

- Recipient: Andrew and any explicitly approved co-facilitator
- Purpose: deliver the complete experience consistently and answer nuanced questions
- Use: rehearse from the editable Google Doc; keep the PDF as an offline backup

## Do not publish

Do not place the workbook, practice pack, safety card, platform record, facilitator playbook, agent files, skill files, or complete toolkit ZIP on the public sales page.
