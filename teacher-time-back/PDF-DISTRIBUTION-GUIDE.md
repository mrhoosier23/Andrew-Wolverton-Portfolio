# Teacher Time-Back Lab: PDF Distribution Guide

## The short rule

Do not send all seven PDFs at once. Each file answers a different question at a different moment. Too many files before the recipient understands the offer will create work instead of reducing it.

## 1. Teacher-Time-Back-Lab-Pilot-Overview.pdf

### What it is

The one-page sales and scope sheet. It explains the problem, the 45 + 90 + 30 format, the participant outcome, safety boundary, cohort size, and founding pilot price.

### Who gets it

- principal or assistant principal;
- district or charter academic leader;
- professional learning lead;
- instructional technology or innovation lead;
- warm introducer who needs a concise attachment.

### How it is used

Attach it to a personalized first outreach email or send it immediately after a warm introduction. The recipient should be able to decide whether a short fit conversation is worthwhile.

### When it is used

First contact or first reply. It is the only attachment needed at this stage.

### It is not

A teacher handout, policy document, curriculum, or detailed implementation guide.

## 2. Leadership-Setup-Guide.pdf

### What it is

The decision worksheet for the 45-minute pre-lab setup. It names the approved environment, green/yellow/red input lanes, human decision line, cohort, starter lanes, day-of readiness, and follow-up data.

### Who gets it

- the sponsor who booked the pilot;
- the leader who owns local AI use guidance;
- a privacy, technology, or instructional lead when appropriate;
- the day-of host.

### How it is used

Use it live during the leadership setup call. Record decisions in plain language. The completed guide becomes the facilitator's local operating brief.

### When it is used

After the school expresses serious interest and before the teacher session is promoted or delivered. Send it with the calendar invitation for the setup call.

### It is not

A substitute for district policy, legal review, cybersecurity review, procurement, or data governance.

## 3. Facilitator-Guide.pdf

### What it is

The delivery map for the 90-minute lab and 30-minute follow-up. It contains timing, facilitator stance, prompts, checkpoints, fallback language, and common participant responses.

### Who gets it

- Andrew;
- an approved co-facilitator;
- optionally, the day-of host if they have an active facilitation role.

### How it is used

Use it to rehearse, time, and facilitate the session. Mark local decisions from the Leadership Setup Guide before the event. Keep it open on a facilitator device or print a working copy.

### When it is used

During preparation, the live lab, and the two-week follow-up. It is not a general participant handout.

## 4. Teacher-Workbook.pdf

### What it is

The participant's main working document. It captures the repeated task, safety boundaries, six-field assistant blueprint, fictional test notes, review routine, and two-week measurement log.

### Who gets it

Every participating teacher. Provide an accessible digital copy and printed copies when useful.

### How it is used

Participants write in it throughout all five build steps. After the lab, it holds the instructions they can use in the approved environment and the evidence they need for a keep, revise, or stop decision.

### When it is used

Send no more than 24 hours before the lab or distribute it at arrival. Reopen it during the 30-minute follow-up.

### It is not

A place to record student names, records, identifiable work, or confidential information.

## 5. Reuse-Planner-Completed-Example.pdf

### What it is

A finished model of the product participants are building. It shows the assistant's one job, allowed sources, prohibited inputs, exact output, quality criteria, refusal behavior, human decision line, and fictional test.

### Who gets it

- teacher participants;
- school leaders who want to see the concrete outcome before booking;
- a co-facilitator.

### How it is used

Show it in the first twelve minutes so beginners can see the destination before learning the parts. Participants may keep it beside the workbook as a reference, but they should adapt the structure to their own safe task.

### When it is used

Optionally during a sales conversation, definitely during the opening demo, and as a reference during the Build step.

### It is not

A one-size-fits-all prompt to copy without checking the school's rules and the teacher's actual task.

## 6. Fictional-Practice-Pack.pdf

### What it is

A made-up Harbor City unit and practice scenario that lets participants test an assistant without student records. It includes a planted unsupported claim so teachers must practice human review.

### Who gets it

Every participant or one copy per table/pair.

### How it is used

Use it as the only practice content during the Practice step. Participants compare the assistant's output against the pack, find the invented bus-stop claim, and revise their instructions.

### When it is used

Distribute or reveal it at the Practice step around minute 65. Withholding it until then keeps earlier attention on task selection and guardrails.

### It is not

A real school, community, student, family, or policy case.

## 7. Safe-Input-and-Review-Cards.pdf

### What it is

Printable quick-reference cards for input screening and output review. They make the safety routine visible at the moment of use.

### Who gets it

- each teacher;
- each table or collaborative group;
- the school host for later display near staff workstations or professional learning materials.

### How it is used

Introduce the cards during the Protect step. Participants use them again during Practice and keep them as a reminder to check approved inputs, identifiers, accuracy, bias, appropriateness, and alignment.

### When it is used

Print and cut before the lab. Place one set at each seat or table. Include the digital PDF in the post-session resource email.

### It is not

A compliance certification or a replacement for local guidance.

## Distribution sequence

| Moment | Recipient | Send or use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First personalized outreach | Decision maker | Pilot Overview only |
| Warm introduction reply | Decision maker and introducer | Pilot Overview only |
| Serious interest or fit call | Decision maker | Pilot Overview and a brief link to the Completed Example if useful |
| Pilot confirmed | Sponsor and responsible leaders | Leadership Setup Guide |
| Internal preparation | Andrew and approved co-facilitator | Facilitator Guide plus completed Leadership Setup Guide |
| Up to 24 hours before the lab | Participants | Teacher Workbook and logistical instructions |
| Opening demonstration | Participants | Completed Reuse Planner Example |
| Protect step | Participants | Safe Input and Review Cards |
| Practice step | Participants | Fictional Practice Pack |
| Post-session resource email | Participants | Workbook, Completed Example, Cards, and local approved-tool reminder |
| Two-week follow-up | Participants and leadership | Workbook measurement pages and cohort-level summary, not individual evaluation data |

## Recommended email attachment limits

- First contact: one PDF.
- Confirmed leadership setup: one or two PDFs.
- Participant pre-lab email: one PDF plus a plain-language logistics note.
- Post-lab resource email: no more than three attachments; a resource hub link is preferable when available.

## Printing and accessibility

- Print the Workbook double-sided only if the writing areas remain easy to use.
- Print the Cards on heavier paper and cut before arrival.
- Keep the Fictional Practice Pack whole so the source and correction challenge stay together.
- Offer tagged or otherwise accessible digital versions when the school requires them.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate green, yellow, red, correct, or incorrect states.
- Test every QR code and link from the actual PDF before sending.

## Version control

- Send only files from `teacher-time-back/output/pdf/`.
- Do not send the Markdown source files to clients unless they request editable source.
- Add the delivery date or version to the file metadata or distribution log, not to the participant's name.
- If policy language changes, regenerate and visually inspect every affected PDF before reuse.
- Keep the local Leadership Setup Guide completed for each school separate from the clean reusable master.

## Final handoff check

Before sending any PDF, confirm:

- the recipient needs this file at this stage;
- the local tool and account language is accurate;
- the file opens and all pages render correctly;
- no student or confidential information appears;
- links work;
- the recipient has one clear next action.
