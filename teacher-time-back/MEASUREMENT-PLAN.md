# Teacher Time-Back Lab: Sales and Pilot Measurement Plan

The page and pilot should be improved from observed behavior, not general enthusiasm.

## Sales-page questions

Track these manually until an analytics setup is deliberately approved:

- Which entry point produced the conversation: direct page, pilot PDF, Time-Back Finder, resource hub, or warm introduction?
- Did the visitor understand the finished product before asking about features?
- Which objection appeared first: time, privacy, approval, price, or uncertainty about the deliverable?
- Which material did the buyer request or open before replying?
- Did the contact become a fit conversation, an internal referral, a later follow-up, or a no-fit decision?

## Founding-pilot funnel

Use `outreach/PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv` to record:

1. Relevant contact identified.
2. Personalized message sent by Andrew.
3. Reply received.
4. Fit conversation held.
5. Leadership setup accepted.
6. Pilot scheduled.
7. Pilot delivered.
8. Two-week follow-up completed.

## Teacher-level pilot measures

For one recurring task, record:

- Estimated minutes before the assistant.
- Minutes with the assistant.
- Correction and review minutes.
- Number of uses during two weeks.
- Useful, partly useful, or discarded.
- Unsafe-input attempts or boundary confusion.
- Keep, revise, or stop.

Do not report gross time saved without subtracting correction and review time. Do not combine teacher estimates into a public claim without documenting the sample and method.

## Learning review after three pilots

- Identify the two strongest first-task lanes.
- Identify the most common approval and privacy confusion.
- Compare in-person and virtual delivery friction.
- Review cohort size and timing.
- Review price against preparation, delivery, follow-up, and conversion.
- Decide which proof can be published with written permission.
- Revise the page, workshop, and outreach from repeated evidence only.
