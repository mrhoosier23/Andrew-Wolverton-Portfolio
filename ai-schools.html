Hands-on professional learning

The Safe AI Assistant Lab

Teachers build a personal assistant for approved, repeatable work. They leave with something useful, a privacy routine, and the judgment to know when the assistant should not be used.

  1. 01

    Choose

    Pick one recurring, permitted task that is taking time and does not require AI to make a student-level decision.

  2. 02

    Protect

    Learn to spot names, IDs, dates, records, sensitive attributes, and combinations of details that can identify a student.

  3. 03

    Build

    Configure the assistant's role, boundaries, task menu, reusable templates, source expectations, and refusal rules inside a school-approved environment.

  4. 04

    Practice

    Test with fictional or student-neutral examples, review the output, correct it, and leave with a repeatable verification routine.

Teachers leave with

A personal assistant blueprint, a safe task menu, reusable workflows, a student-information redaction checklist, and a human-review routine. School policy and approved-tool rules always control.
