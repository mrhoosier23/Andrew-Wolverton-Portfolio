The Monday Morning Test

Would your staff give the same answer?

Answer for the school or program as it operates today. This reveals implementation gaps. It does not score compliance.

01Can staff name which AI tools are approved, for whom, and for what tasks?
02Would two teachers give the same answer about AI on a student assignment?
03Does everyone know what information must never enter an unapproved tool?

The AI Clarity Sprint

Andrew helps leadership turn the guidance you already have into consistent staff decisions, communication, and an editable system your team can keep using.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Review the guidance already in use and find where staff, leadership, or families are getting stuck.

  2. 02

    Align

    Facilitate the unresolved questions, decision owners, and tradeoffs that need leadership agreement.

  3. 03

    Build

    Create a staff-ready quick guide, decision path, communication kit, and small pilot or rollout plan.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Leadership approves the materials. Your team keeps an editable system for the next question.
