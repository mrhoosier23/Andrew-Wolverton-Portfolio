MOBILE POLISH UPDATE SUMMARY

ABOUT HEADSHOT
The mobile About image now fills its card using a deliberate crop and focal position. The empty area above Andrew's head is removed while the desktop treatment remains unchanged.

WORKSPACE AVATAR
The home avatar is enlarged and shifted upward inside its mobile card. This removes the unused area above the character while keeping the figure caption intact.

LIVE MUSIC BOOKING AVATAR
The singing avatar already used by the desktop Live Music section is now visible on mobile. It appears as a contained stage portrait between the service details and price card.

EXPANDABLE TOOLKIT
The five tool categories now use accessible native details and summary controls. On mobile, the first category opens initially and opening a new category closes the previous category. On desktop, every category opens automatically.

AUDIO WAVEFORM
The waveform bars can now shrink below two pixels and are clipped inside the player. Mobile generates 44 bars instead of 84, preventing the waveform from extending beyond the card.

CASE-STUDY SPACING
The mobile case-study copy now has 18 pixels of internal side padding and 24 pixels of bottom padding. The Result box also has more internal space and a softer corner radius.

STUDIO KEYS GLOW
The mobile desk shortcut for Studio Keys now has a restrained aqua and purple pulse with a moving sheen. Inside Studio Keys, the mobile view buttons, mode controls, transport buttons, pads, and sound gate have clearer luminous edges and stronger pressed feedback.
