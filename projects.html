Founding partner case study

Discovery Sound Garden is an organization, not a single deliverable.

The case study below separates the layers of nonprofit building that can disappear when DSG is shown only as a website or branding project.

01

Mission and program model

Defined a pathway connecting music learning, creative confidence, recording access, performance, and community participation.

02

Curriculum and programs

Developed adult musicianship classes, community ensemble concepts, workshops, and participant journeys that can grow over time.

03

Governance and leadership

Built board materials, roles, operating expectations, planning documents, and the systems needed to move from founding-partner idea to accountable organization.

04

Fundraising and partnerships

Created grant, donor, sponsorship, and board-led fundraising tools while translating the mission into concrete outcomes supporters can understand.

05

Brand and audience

Built the brand, website, registration pathways, campaign language, program messaging, and early audience development.

06

Operations and infrastructure

Established repeatable workflows for program planning, communications, contacts, documentation, and organizational decision-making.

What this demonstrates

Program design, nonprofit leadership, fundraising, communications, and systems thinking in one founding-partner-led body of work.
