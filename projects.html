Porch Stomp

The Problem

A growing festival needed a clearer digital home for artists, stages, visitors, supporters, and years of history.

The Diagnosis

Too many important pathways were competing at once, especially on mobile, while the lineup needed room to become a useful archive.

The Fix

Reorganized the information architecture, strengthened mobile readability, and built a lineup browser designed to expand.

The Result

A clearer festival experience that supports discovery now and a growing archive later.

What Andrew Actually Did

UX strategy, information architecture, content structure, responsive design, custom interface planning, and front-end implementation.

Discovery Sound Garden

The Problem

A new music nonprofit had programs, partnerships, participation, and donor needs, but no single understandable route through them.

The Diagnosis

The mission made sense internally, but each audience needed a different entry point and a visible path from learning to creating and sharing.

The Fix

Built one digital ecosystem around audience-specific journeys that connect education, recording access, performance, participation, partnerships, and support.

The Result

A clearer public pathway for students, emerging artists, returning adults, partners, and donors without flattening the organization’s range.

What Andrew Actually Did

Mission translation, audience mapping, program architecture, information architecture, UX, copy structure, website implementation, and founder-level operations.

Yolélé Ingredients

The Problem

Commercial buyers needed to evaluate an ingredient opportunity quickly, but the most useful answers were not organized around their decision.

The Diagnosis

The experience needed to answer product, format, application, scale, sourcing, and inquiry questions before asking buyers to absorb the broader brand story.

The Fix

Reorganized the buyer journey around commercial questions, practical applications, available formats, sourcing credibility, and a clearer inquiry route.

The Result

A more direct evaluation path that helps the right buyers understand fit and move toward a useful conversation.

What Andrew Actually Did

B2B journey mapping, information architecture, content strategy, UX structure, inquiry-form architecture, and front-end experience planning.

02 / Nonprofit leadership

Building a music nonprofit from the ground up.

Discovery Sound Garden is an organization, not a single deliverable. This case study shows work across program design, governance, fundraising, communications, partnerships, curriculum, and operational systems.

  • Program Design
  • Governance
  • Fundraising
  • Communications
  • Operations
  • Partnerships

The fix was the organization around the mission.

01

Mission and program model

Defined a pathway connecting music learning, creative confidence, recording access, performance, and community participation.

02

Curriculum and programs

Developed adult musicianship classes, community ensemble concepts, workshops, and participant journeys that can grow over time.

03

Governance and leadership

Built board materials, roles, operating expectations, planning documents, and the systems needed to move from founding-partner idea to accountable organization.

04

Fundraising and partnerships

Created grant, donor, sponsorship, and board-led fundraising tools while translating the mission into concrete outcomes supporters can understand.

05

Brand and audience

Built the brand, website, registration pathways, campaign language, program messaging, and early audience development.

06

Operations and infrastructure

Established repeatable workflows for program planning, communications, contacts, documentation, and organizational decision-making.

What this demonstrates

Program design, nonprofit leadership, fundraising, communications, and systems thinking in one founding-partner-led body of work.
