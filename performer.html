What I do live

The set can move.
The room moves with it.

I perform on vocals, guitar, harmonica, and piano. Depending on the night, the music can move through roots and Americana, folk, country, bluegrass, classic rock, the Great American Songbook, pop, funk, soul, and musical theatre.

I am most at home when the set feels alive: musicians trading the spotlight, room for improvisation, and enough flexibility to follow what is working with the audience.

About Andrew / Mr. Hoosier

Singer first.
Genre boundaries optional.

Andrew Wolverton is a New York-based singer, multi-instrumentalist, and actor-musician performing on vocals, guitar, harmonica, and piano. His musical life has never stayed in one lane. A set might move from bluegrass and country into classic rock, musical theatre, folk, the Great American Songbook, funk, soul, or a pop song rebuilt for a live band.

Andrew performs both as a solo artist and in collaborative projects including Rooftop Ramblers. His recent live work also includes performances with Lake Effect Bluegrass at Outlaw Saloon.

What ties the different styles together is the live-room experience: a direct vocal, musicians listening to each other, room for improvisation, and a frontman who wants the audience in the moment rather than watching from a distance.
