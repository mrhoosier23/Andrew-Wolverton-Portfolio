About Andrew

I have spent most of my career helping people make something together.

I started as a theater and choir teacher in Indiana, moved into arts management and producing, and built a career around the communication, fundraising, digital work, and practical systems that help creative organizations keep going.

That path includes classrooms, community theater, the Singing Hoosiers, Carnegie Mellon, professional producing, New York arts organizations, live music, and founding Discovery Sound Garden.

The route here

Indiana roots, arts management, New York, and building something of my own.

This is the personal context behind the mix of education, producing, fundraising, digital work, music, and systems throughout the portfolio.

Lafayette and Indiana

Learning to build with the people already in the room.

Teaching theater and choir meant coordinating students, families, rehearsals, performances, sponsors, schedules, and a thousand practical details before opening night. Community and civic theater reinforced that ambitious creative work still has to function locally and humanely.

Indiana University

A music community that continued after graduation.

Indiana University and the Singing Hoosiers shaped Andrew as a performer and community member. He later served the Singing Hoosiers Alumni Council in communications leadership, as board president, and now as President Emeritus, supporting alumni connection, fundraising, and long-term stewardship.

Carnegie Mellon

Connecting artistic ideas to audiences, resources, and operations.

The Master of Arts Management program expanded the work into fundraising, finance, marketing, organizational strategy, audience development, and the structures that allow artistic projects to survive beyond one event.

Professional producing

Seeing established theater production from closer range.

Associate producing work connected to a Kathleen Turner production under Davenport added experience with professional producing, recognizable talent, commercial expectations, and the coordination required around a major theatrical project.

New York City

Fundraising, marketing, media, web work, and live performance.

Work in New York has included development and marketing for Red Bull Theater, digital and media projects for arts organizations, small businesses, and performers, live music, audio production, and the ongoing challenge of helping small teams do sophisticated work without enormous departments.

Discovery Sound Garden

Building the organization Andrew wanted to exist.

Discovery Sound Garden brings together accessible music learning, recording, performance, community, and practical nonprofit infrastructure. Founding it required program development, governance, fundraising, communications, partnerships, curriculum, and the willingness to keep refining how the mission becomes a real participant experience.

What matters to me

The work should make participation more possible.

These are not generic brand values. They are the principles that connect the different parts of Andrew's career.

Welcoming music education

Adults and young people deserve serious learning environments that do not punish them for starting late, returning after time away, or learning differently.

Community arts with ambition

Accessible and community-centered work can still be musically, artistically, and operationally rigorous.

Systems that reduce confusion

Technology should remove repeated friction, clarify decisions, and leave people more capable, not more dependent on an opaque setup.

Human judgment in AI work

AI can accelerate research, organization, drafting, and testing. Final judgment, source checking, design direction, and accountability remain human responsibilities.

Long-term creative communities

Alumni, participants, artists, and supporters should have meaningful ways to remain connected beyond a single event or transaction.

Small teams deserve strong tools

Arts and nonprofit organizations should not need corporate-sized departments to communicate clearly, raise support, or maintain useful digital systems.
